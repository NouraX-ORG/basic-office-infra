# Welcome to Nourax Workspace

Your files live in **NoDrive**. From the top bar you can also open:

| App | What it is for |
|---|---|
| NoDrive | Files and folders, share links, version history |
| ONLYOFFICE | Documents, spreadsheets and presentations (Word, Excel and PowerPoint files) |
| NoCalendar | Calendars and events, synced to your phone |
| NoContacts | Address book, synced to your phone |
| NoBoard | Kanban boards with a Gantt timeline |

The **NoMail** folder is used by Nourax mail: big attachments you send are stored in
`NoMail/Sent`, and attachments you save from received mail go to `NoMail/Received`.

You can delete this file.
